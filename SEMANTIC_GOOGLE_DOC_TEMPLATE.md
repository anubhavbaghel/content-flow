### FAQs
**Q: How often should I book Chimney Sweeping?**  
**A:** That depends on how often you use your fireplace or stove, but regular sweeping is important if you burn fuel throughout the colder months.  

**Q: Can Chimney Sweeping help stop smoky smells in the room?**  
**A:** Yes, it often can. Old soot and chimney deposits can cause unpleasant odours, and sweeping helps improve conditions inside the chimney.  

**Q: Will a sweep show if there is a problem with my chimney?**  
**A:** Sweeping can reveal warning signs such as heavy build-up or poor draw.
